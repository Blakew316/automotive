Before you come in

A few things that help.

None of these are required, but each one helps us track down the problem faster.

  1. Note the symptoms

    When does it happen: cold starts, at highway speed, turning or braking? A short video of a noise is a big help.

  2. Check the dash

    Note which warning lights are on and whether they’re steady or flashing. A flashing check engine light usually signals a severe misfire that can overheat the catalytic converter, so avoid driving it hard and call us.

  3. Bring every key we’ll need

    If your wheels have locking lug nuts, leave the wheel lock key with us. We need it for tire, brake and suspension work.

  4. Share the history

    Mileage, recent repairs and service records all help, especially if the problem started after other work.

  5. For mowers and small engines

    Note the make and model, and how long the fuel has been sitting. Stale fuel is a common cause of hard starting.

Contact FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is the best way to make sure we have time set aside for your vehicle or equipment. If something comes up suddenly, call us and we’ll tell you what we can do.

Can you give me a price over the phone?

For routine maintenance we can often give you a ballpark. Anything that needs diagnosis we’ll need to see first. We’ll inspect it, then give you a written estimate before any work begins.

What’s the fastest way to reach you?

Calling during shop hours is fastest. Messages and emails are great for questions that aren’t urgent, and we’ll get back to you as soon as we can.
