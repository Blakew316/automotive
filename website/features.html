    Good to know

    • Needs: most parts need nothing extra. Texting, card payments, email from your address, QuickBooks, AI and connected cars need Shop Cloud and your own account with that service.
    • One browser until you connect: without Shop Cloud, data lives only in that browser. Export a backup file from Settings now and then.
    • Roles live on the device: they decide which pages show, not who can read what on the server. What each role can open
    • Billed by each service: Stripe, Twilio, Resend, Anthropic, Smartcar and Intuit charge your shop at their own rates.
    • Not repair information: it doesn’t replace ALLDATA, Mitchell or factory service information.

    Questions

    About the features

    All questions
    Do I have to use all of it?

    No. Features that need Shop Cloud or an outside account stay off until you connect them. Use the parts you need and leave the rest switched off.

    What works on one device with nothing connected?

    Repair orders, estimates and PDFs, inspections (on the counter tablet or as a report file), the workflow board, the tech clock, parts and POs, reports and CSV exports, the vehicle database, VIN decoder, service library and import.

    Does it work on one computer?

    Yes. Data lives in that browser until you use Shop Cloud. Clearing browser data erases it, so export a backup file from Settings now and then.

    Does it replace ALLDATA or Mitchell?

    No. OEM and paid repair information stays separate; the app links to OEM portals, and those subscriptions are your own. The built-in vehicle database, VIN decoder and service library help with everyday lookups: vehicle configurations, generated system diagrams, 725 generic trouble codes and shop calculators.
