What the demo form collects

This site has one form: the demo request. It only collects what you type or pick, in three short steps.

1Your shop

  • Topic what you’re asking
  • Shop name for a demo request
  • Your role optional
  • Locations and bays optional
  • System you use now optional

2What to see

Features: demo requests only

  • Features you tick optional
  • Your message optional; in step 1 for other topics

3Contact

  • Your name required
  • Email required
  • Phone if you pick a call or text
  • How and when to reach you optional

Nothing else about you is added to the request. The services that receive it may also log your IP address and browser, as web services do. Please keep card numbers, passwords and your customers’ details out of the message box.

Where it goes

When you press Send request, your request takes the first route below that works.

  1. First

    To WPI Driveline’s own copy of the system

    It goes to the Shop Cloud inbox of the copy of the WPI Driveline Shop Management System that WPI Driveline runs for itself. When one of our signed-in devices picks it up (a device checks about every minute while it’s open), your name, email, phone and shop name are saved as a contact with an unread message, and the inbox copy is deleted.

  2. If that fails

    To Netlify Forms, where available

    If the inbox can’t be reached and this site is being served from Netlify, the request goes to Netlify Forms instead. On other hosts this step is skipped.

  3. If that fails too

    Nothing is sent

    Your answers stay in the form so you can try again. When an email address is listed on the demo page, you’re offered an email instead, with your answers filled in.

How long we keep it

We keep your request, saved as a contact and a message in our copy of the system, for as long as we need it to follow up with you. Ask us through the contact options below and we’ll delete it. Copies in the system’s change history and nightly backups clear on their own within six months.

No analytics or ad trackers

This site doesn’t use analytics or ad trackers. Pages, fonts and images all load from this site, and no outside scripts load with them.

  • No analytics
  • No ad trackers
  • No third-party scripts
  • No cookies set by this site

The one thing these pages remember

Press Pause animations and your browser keeps that choice in its local storage, under wpi-site:motion, so the next page stays still too. Press Play animations and it’s cleared. (If you open the sample shop, it keeps its own data in this browser. See The sample shop.)

The service that hosts these pages may keep standard request logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the page asked for, to keep the site running.

Faster pages

So pages open at once, your browser may start loading a page from this site before you click: usually when you point at or press a link, and in some browsers when you move to it with the keyboard or scroll past it.

  • Pages on this siteMay load a moment early Early
  • The app and the sample shopNever loaded early On open

Nothing from the app is set up on your device until you choose to open it.

The sample shop

Try the sample shop opens Main Street Auto Service, a made-up shop with made-up customers, right in your browser. There’s no sign-up and no sign-in.

Saved in
This browser, on this deviceIn its storage (IndexedDB and local storage), with the app’s files so it opens offline.
Synced to
NowhereThe sample shop isn’t signed in to Shop Cloud.
To remove it
Clear this site’s dataIn your browser’s settings, under site or website data.

Your edits stay in that browser between visits. On a device that already has a shop saved, the same link opens that shop instead. Customer pages you open from it, such as the booking link, are live pages: what you type there is sent to WPI Driveline’s Shop Cloud.

A few tools look things up online. Auto Diagnosis, for example, asks NHTSA, the U.S. vehicle-safety agency, about recalls and complaints for a vehicle’s year, make and model.

Shops using the system

If your shop runs the WPI Driveline Shop Management System, your customers’ records belong to your shop. They live on your shop’s devices and, with Shop Cloud, in your shop’s private Shop Cloud project, and in the outside services your shop connects through it (texting, card payments, email, QuickBooks, connected cars and AI).

This notice covers this website, the demo form and the sample shop. It doesn’t cover those records. If you reached this page from a repair shop’s text or link, ask that shop about your information.

Good to know

  • Covers: this website, the demo form and the sample shop. It doesn’t cover the records a shop keeps about its own customers.
  • Sample shop: it lives in one browser on one device. Clearing site data erases it, and it doesn’t follow you to another device.
  • Animation setting: remembered per browser. A private window forgets it when you close it.
  • Demo form: needs your name and email, plus your shop’s name for a demo request. Your phone number is needed only if you ask for a call or a text.
