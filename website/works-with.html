Shop Cloud

Shop Cloud: what links it all together

Shop Cloud is the cloud side of WPI Driveline Shop Management System: a private Supabase project that holds one shop’s shared records, logins and files and runs its server functions.

It’s also required for every outside service on this page except the built-in NHTSA data. Their keys are stored and used on your Shop Cloud server, and a signed-in shop device records what comes back: payments, approvals and check-ins. A device checks about every minute while it’s open.

Without Shop Cloud

One device works on its own. Repair orders, inspections on the counter tablet, the board, the tech clock, parts, reports and the vehicle data all work, and nothing is shared.

Outside services

What each one turns on

Each one is separate, so connect only what your shop will use. All but the built-in NHTSA data run through Shop Cloud on your own accounts, and the app keeps working without any of them.

Needs your account

Stripe

Card payments

Adds
Pay links for the exact balance, Stripe Checkout, Stripe Reader S700 and BBPOS WisePOS E counter readers, and refunds from the RO.
You need
Shop Cloud and your own Stripe account, at Stripe’s fees.
Without it
Record payments by hand, or put Square, PayPal, Venmo or Cash App links on invoices.
Payments & card readers
Needs your account

Twilio

Texting and calls

Adds
Business texting with picture messages, calls that ring up to five of your phones, voicemail, missed-call texts, reminders sent by the server, and the AI receptionist (with your Anthropic key).
You need
Shop Cloud and your own number with A2P 10DLC or toll-free verification. Billed per text and minute.
Without it
Texts open in your device’s Messages app and are still logged on the customer.
Texting, calls & email
Needs your account

Resend

Email from your domain

Adds
Email from your own domain (an email sent from a repair order can carry its estimate, invoice or receipt as a PDF), Delivered and Opened status once its webhook is set up, and the daily summary email.
You need
Shop Cloud, a Resend account (free tier and paid plans) and DNS records on your domain.
Without it
Emails open in your device’s Mail app.
Email from your shop
Needs your account

Accounting

Connected cars

Shop Cloud, a Smartcar account (free developer tier, paid beyond it) and the car owner’s OK.

No AI drafts, test plans or AI receptionist. With Twilio, calls ring your phones and go to voicemail. Everything else works the same.

How integration keys are kept

Good to know

  • Needs: Shop Cloud for every outside service except the built-in NHTSA data, plus your own account with each provider you connect.
  • Each provider bills your shop directly at its own rates. We don’t publish prices; ask how Shop Cloud is set up and billed in a demo request.
  • Payments, approvals and check-ins that come back land on your signed-in shop devices. A device checks about every minute while it’s open.
  • U.S. carriers block business texts until A2P 10DLC or toll-free verification is done.
  • QuickBooks runs against the sandbox company until Intuit reviews your app. Daily QuickBooks posting and the daily summary email’s numbers come from an owner or manager device opened that day.
  • Counter readers are the Stripe Reader S700 and BBPOS WisePOS E. Bluetooth readers and Tap to Pay on a phone aren’t supported; record those payments by hand.
  • Only the owner can add or change integration keys.

FAQ

Integration questions

Do I need all of them?

No. Each one is separate, so connect only what your shop will use. Repair orders, inspections, the board, parts and reports don’t need any of them.

Do outside services work without Shop Cloud?

No. Their keys live on your Shop Cloud server, and every call to them goes through your shop’s server functions. NHTSA vehicle data is the exception: it’s built in and needs no key.

Who do I pay for texting?

Twilio, directly. Texts and minutes are billed to your own Twilio account.

Can I use Square at the counter?

As a payment link on invoices and the customer report. The counter card readers are Stripe: the Stripe Reader S700 and the BBPOS WisePOS E.

Who can change keys?

The owner. Once a key is saved, the screen shows only that it’s set, its last four characters and when it changed. How keys are kept
