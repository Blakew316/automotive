Service FAQ

Before you book.

A few things people ask us most. Still unsure? Call and describe what’s happening. We’re happy to help.

All FAQs Makes we service
What if I don’t know which service I need?

That’s normal, and it’s what diagnosis is for. Describe what you’re noticing when you book or call, or try the symptom finder on this page. We inspect the vehicle and explain what we find before any work begins.

Do you work on my make and model?

Chances are good. We service domestic, Asian and European cars, SUVs, light trucks and vans, plus many small engine brands. See the makes we service, or call with your year, make and model to confirm.

What happens if you find something else while you’re working?

We stop and call you. We explain what we found, why it matters and what it costs, and nothing gets added to the job without your OK.
