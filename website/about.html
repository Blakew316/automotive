What’s in a name

Named for what keeps you moving.

A driveline carries power from the engine to the wheels. Keeping all of it working together, on the car in your driveway and the equipment in your garage, is the whole job. Here’s what that means when you pull in.

01

Engine to wheels

Maintenance, diagnostics and repairs under one roof: oil changes and brakes, check-engine lights, engine and cooling work, A/C, tires, suspension, electrical and more. One shop that can look after the whole vehicle.

02

For the long run

Care, not just repair. We look after cars, trucks, SUVs and vans for the long run, and we’ll tell you what’s coming up so fewer things catch you off guard.

03

And small engines, too

The mowers, riding tractors, generators, pressure washers and saws that keep your home and your work running get the same careful attention as your car.

What we value

How we like to work.

Four simple ideas guide every job, whether it’s a pickup truck or a push mower.

Honesty

We recommend what your vehicle or equipment actually needs, and we’ll tell you when something can wait.

Clear communication

Plain language, no jargon. You’ll know what we found, what it costs and why it matters before we start.

Careful workmanship

We diagnose before we replace, follow the manufacturer’s specs and check our work before you get your keys.

Respect for your time

We give you a realistic idea of timing up front and keep you posted if anything changes along the way.

What to expect

What to expect when you visit.

Car, truck or chainsaw, every job follows the same simple routine. No pressure, no surprises on the bill.

Your visit, step by stepHow every job moves through the shop
Every visit

  • We listen.

    Tell us what you’re hearing, seeing or feeling. The details help us find the problem faster.

  • We inspect.

    A technician checks it out and tracks down the cause, not just the symptom.

  • We explain, with a written estimate.

    Parts and labor spelled out, plus what’s urgent and what can wait.

  • We call before anything extra.

    Find something else along the way? We call you first. Nothing gets added without your OK.

  • We show you the old parts.

    Just ask, and we’ll show you what came off and why it needed replacing.

  • We keep records.

    Your invoice lists the work we did, so you have a service history for your vehicle or equipment.

Questions at any step? Just ask. We’re happy to explain.
