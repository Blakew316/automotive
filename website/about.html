The name

What’s in a name

Two words in the name, and the bar under the logo, say what the product does for a shop.

The driveline

From the counter to the books

A driveline carries an engine’s power through the gearbox and the driveshaft to the wheels. In your shop, every car rides one from check-in to paid, and each stop is part of the same system.

Principles

How it’s made

Four rules the product is built to. Each one shows up somewhere you can see it.

Your shop’s name first

Customer pages and PDFs lead with your shop’s name. The product signs off in one small “Powered by” line at the bottom.

One record per job

One repair order carries a job from estimate to paid receipt, and every feature works from the same customer list.

Nothing invented

Auto Diagnosis takes vehicle-specific facts only from NHTSA data and your own repair orders, and its AI test plan is a draft for your tech to check. Every mockup on this site is labelled as an example.

Plain about limits

Every feature page says what it needs and what it doesn’t do, in a “Good to know” box like the one further down.

Technology

Under the hood

A web app on every device, with Shop Cloud behind it. Each device keeps the whole shop, and Shop Cloud joins them up when you want them to share.

The app is the same on the counter PC, the bay iPad and the owner’s phone. There’s nothing to install from a store and no server in the back office to look after.

Spec sheet

  • Installs from the browser

    A web app that installs from the browser and works offline, on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and PC.

  • The whole shop on each device

    Each device keeps the shop’s records in its own storage, so the counter keeps going when the internet drops.

  • Shop Cloud, for one shop

    Shop Cloud runs on a private Supabase project for one shop: database, live sync, file storage, server functions and an encrypted key vault.

  • Tested on every pull request

    Unit tests, server-function tests with the outside providers mocked, and browser tests that include an iPhone-sized layout. CI runs them on every pull request.

Sign-in, roles, keys and backups are on Security & your data.

Good to know

  • Not a store app: it installs from the browser. It isn’t in the App Store or Google Play.
  • Without Shop Cloud: records live only in that one browser, and clearing browser data erases them, so export backups from Settings. Photos and video stay on that device and aren’t in the backup file.
  • Needs: Shop Cloud to share records between devices and for customer links.
  • Your own accounts: texting, card payments, email, QuickBooks, connected cars and AI run through Shop Cloud on your shop’s own Twilio, Stripe, Resend, Intuit, Smartcar and Anthropic accounts, which bill you directly.
  • Prices: we don’t publish prices. Tell us about your shop in a demo request, and ask how Shop Cloud is set up and billed for you.
