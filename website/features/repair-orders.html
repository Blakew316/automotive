Pricing

Priced the way your shop prices

Your rates, your parts matrix and your menu of canned jobs, set once in Settings and used on every estimate.

Every kind of line

Labor, part, fee and sublet lines. Shop supplies as a percent of labor with a dollar cap. Discounts in % or $, taken off before tax. Sales tax that can include labor, and tax-exempt customers.

Canned jobs

A service menu with categories and menu hours. Search it and add a job to the RO, or build a blank service line by line. The same canned jobs feed online booking and fleet PM plans.

A parts markup matrix

Sell price = cost × (1 + markup), with tiers by unit cost. A small part carries a bigger markup than a big one, and nobody does the math at the counter.

Each service on its own terms

Approve or decline each service on its own, with its own cause and correction and its own assigned tech. The customer's concern and the promised time sit on the RO.

Your rates

Shop labor rate, default tech cost per hour, sales tax and shop supplies, set once. Each location can override its labor rate and sales tax.

Your terms on paper

Your warranty text and your estimate and invoice terms print at the bottom of every document.

Job profitability

Know the profit before you say yes

Every service shows GP $, GP % and GP per labor hour after parts, sublet and the assigned tech's pay.

The profit meter on the RO adds it up for the whole job and marks your GP target. A service under the target shows amber or red, so a thin estimate gets a second look before it goes out.

Labor & parts memory

Add a job and the RO shows how often your shop has done it, the hours it usually took and the parts that went in. It looks at the same model first, then the same make, then any vehicle. One tap applies the hours or adds the parts with last cost and vendor.

GP uses each tech's pay rate, or your default tech cost per hour. You set the GP target with your shop goals in Reports.

On every RO

The details that save the margin

The small things that leak money between the estimate and the receipt, kept on the same record.

Comebacks you can see

Mark an RO as a comeback and it links back to the original RO and the tech who did the work. The comebacks and no-charge report adds them up by tech.

Any service can be no charge as shop warranty, comeback or goodwill. The customer pays nothing for it, and its cost still counts against the job's profit.

Core charges that come back

Put a core charge on a part line and it's tracked from owed to returned to credited.

Parts → Cores lists every core still waiting to go back, so a battery core in the back room doesn't turn into money you never see again.

Parts, inventory & tires

How they're getting around

Each RO records how the customer gets around while you have the car: waiting, drop-off, shuttle ride, rental car or a loaner.

A loaner is checked out and back in with odometer and fuel and a signed agreement, so a loaner that comes back low is on the record.

Real PDFs from the RO

Estimates, work orders, invoices and receipts are real PDFs, built in the browser from the RO. Print them or download them. On an iPhone or iPad the PDF goes to the share sheet for Mail, Messages or Files.

Email from your shop's own address attaches the PDF for you. The AI assistant and Auto Diagnosis open right from the RO.

Email from your shop's address uses Shop Cloud with your own Resend account.

Good to know

  • Needs: nothing extra. Repair orders, the pricing matrix, the profit figures and the PDFs are built in.
  • Sending from your shop: texting or emailing from your shop's own number or address uses Shop Cloud with your own Twilio or Resend account. Without them, texts and emails open in your device's Messages or Mail app instead.
  • Part costs come from your inventory or what you type in. Supplier lookup opens the supplier's own site; there's no live supplier pricing inside the app. See Parts, inventory & tires.
  • Profit stays inside: GP uses each tech's pay rate, or your default tech cost per hour. Estimates and invoices show prices, never your costs or GP.

FAQ

Repair order questions

All questions
Can I set my own markup tiers?

Yes, by unit cost. Each tier has its own markup, and a part's sell price is its cost × (1 + markup).

Tax-exempt fleets and labor tax?

Both supported. Mark a business account tax exempt with its tax ID, and turn on tax on labor if your state taxes it.

How is GP figured?

After parts, sublet and the assigned tech's pay. Labor cost uses that tech's pay rate, or your default tech cost per hour when no tech is set.

Can each location have its own rates?

Yes, labor rate and sales tax. Estimates and invoices also use that location's own details.

Do customers see my margins?

No. Estimates, invoices and the customer's report show prices, not your costs or GP.
