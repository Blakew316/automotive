Where fleet billing stalls

Fleet work is steady work. The billing is where it slows down: a missing number, a spreadsheet of who owes what, and a fleet manager who has to call to find out.

  • Invoices sent back for a missing PO.
  • Aging kept in a spreadsheet.
  • The fleet manager calling for status.

Account setup

Accounts set up the way fleets buy

Turn any business customer into an account. Set the rules once, and every visit, invoice and statement follows them.

Terms and limits

Terms from due on receipt to Net 7, 15, 30, 45 or 60, a credit limit, and a not-to-exceed amount per visit.

POs, tax and billing

Required PO numbers, tax exemption with the certificate number, a billing email and a note on every invoice.

Units

Units with a unit number and a driver or department, so each invoice says which truck it was.

Charge to account

Charge to account asks for the PO when it’s required and warns when the credit limit is exceeded. Invoices print the PO, unit, terms and due date.

  • Charge it and the truck goes home. The invoice waits on the account, due on its terms.
  • Over the limit? You can still charge it, or take a payment instead.

Preventive maintenance

PM plans that start the RO

Presets: oil & filter every 5,000 mi or 6 months, tire rotation every 7,500 mi, annual safety inspection. Each unit shows as up to date, due soon or overdue, and one click starts a PM repair order with the due jobs.

  • Due by whichever comes first, the miles or the months.
  • Due soon means within 30 days, or within 10% of the mileage interval (at least 500 mi).
  • Done somewhere else? Record the date and miles by hand.
What the fleet manager sees

Receivables

Aging, statements and one check for many invoices

  • Aging in current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days, per account and shop-wide.
  • Receive payment applies one check or ACH across several invoices, oldest first.
  • Printable or PDF statements with aging, open invoices and recent payments, or email them to the billing contact.

Past-due fleet invoices also show up in the Needs attention list on the Today screen, so nobody has to remember to look.

Fleet portal

A portal for the fleet manager

A private link with every unit’s maintenance status, what’s in the shop (with live status and estimate links), open invoices, the balance, and Pay and Book buttons. No costs, margins or notes. You can turn it off. It uses Shop Cloud.

  • No login. Text or email the link to the fleet manager from the account.
  • Your signed-in shop devices keep it current, and an open portal checks for changes every minute.
  • Invoices show totals only, never line costs.

Good to know

  • Needs: Shop Cloud for the fleet portal. Everything else on this page works without it.
  • Paying from the portal: the Pay button opens the online payment link you set in Settings: a Stripe Payment Link, a Square link, or PayPal.me, Venmo or Cash App with the balance filled in. Without a link, the portal has no Pay button.
  • Booking from the portal: Schedule service shows when online booking is turned on.
  • Statements print or save as a PDF from the statement page. Emailing one sends it as text, from your shop’s address with Shop Cloud and Resend, or through your own mail app.
  • PM status is worked out from each unit’s mileage on file and its last PM, taken from repair orders here or a date you enter by hand.

FAQ

Fleet questions

All questions
Can I require a PO?

Yes. Turn on PO number required for the account, and Charge to account won’t go through until a PO is entered.

Can one check pay several invoices?

Yes, oldest first. You can also pick just the invoices the remittance lists.

Does the fleet manager need a login?

No, a private link. You can turn it off from the account at any time.

What does the portal hide?

Costs, margins and notes. Invoices show their totals, not line costs.
