Workflow board, Today & tech clock

See every car in the shop at a glance.

A drag-and-drop board with every open repair order by stage, a morning screen that tells you what needs someone right now, and a phone-sized time clock for every tech.

Request a demo Open the board in the sample shop
Drag, swipe, undo Promised times on every card One-tap job timers

Sound familiar?

Where cars get lost

  • The whiteboard is out of date by 9 AM.
  • “Where’s the Camry?”
  • Promised times slip and nobody notices until the customer calls.
  • Techs walk to the counter for every update.

One board, one list, one clock

One board for the whole shop, a morning list that builds itself, and a time clock that moves the card when the tech starts.

Workflow board

The board

Every open repair order sits in the stage it’s in. Move the card when the work moves.

Five stages

Estimate, Approved, In Progress, Waiting on Parts and Ready for Pickup. Closed ROs drop off the board.

Drag, or swipe

Drag cards on a desktop or iPad. On a phone, swipe between stages and move a card from its ••• menu.

Undo on every move

Each move shows a toast with an Undo button. A wrong drop is one tap to fix.

Filter and search

Show everyone or one technician. Search by RO, customer, vehicle or plate.

Cards that say enough

The tech, the promised time, services done out of total, and parts still on order. A promised time turns red once it has passed.

Time-stamped

Every stage change is time-stamped. With Shop Cloud, those times drive the customer’s live status page.

Today dashboard

Today: the shop’s morning screen

Today is the app’s home screen. Five totals sit across the top: in the shop, billed today, awaiting approval, ready for pickup and receivables. Under them, Needs attention builds itself from what’s already in the system. It watches for:

  • Cars past their promise time
  • ROs waiting on parts
  • Estimates with no follow-up after 3 hours
  • Ready cars with a balance due
  • Past-due fleet invoices
  • POs due today or overdue
  • Low stock
  • Booking requests to confirm
  • Today’s self check-ins
  • Unread messages and missed calls to return

Also on the screen: today’s appointments with a one-tap Book appointment button, an activity feed, and shortcut tiles with live counts. The tiles are filtered to what the signed-in role may open.

Tech time clock

A time clock made for the bay

Each tech gets a phone-sized screen with their own work on it. Big buttons, short labels, and nothing they have to hunt for.

  • Clock in and out for the shift, with time on the clock today.
  • Assigned jobs, plus open jobs a tech can claim and start.
  • One-tap job timers. Starting a job clocks the tech in and moves an Approved RO to In Progress.
  • Mark services done and write the cause and correction.
  • Take a photo or video in the bay, straight onto the RO.
  • One tap to the inspection, Auto Diagnosis, and the vehicle’s diagrams, parts and repair guides.
  • On phones, techs get their own tab bar: Clock, Board, Orders, Diagnose.

A Right now board shows who’s on which job. Efficiency and productivity come from real clock time:

Efficiency
flagged hours ÷ hours on jobs
Productivity
hours on jobs ÷ hours on the clock

Good to know

  • Needs: nothing extra. The board, Today and the time clock work on a single device.
  • One live board for the shop: keeping the counter, bay tablets and phones on the same board uses Shop Cloud. Without it, each device keeps its own records.
  • Moving a card doesn’t text the customer. You choose when to send an update.
  • The time clock counts hours and exports them. Payroll itself runs in your payroll service.

Questions

Board and time clock questions

Can techs move cards from a phone?

Yes. Swipe between stages, then use the ••• menu on a card to move it. On a desktop or iPad, drag it.

What if someone moves the wrong card?

Undo. Every move shows a toast with an Undo button, and one tap puts the card back.

Does moving a card text the customer?

No. With Shop Cloud, the move updates the live status page if you’ve shared it. Sending the status-update text template is one tap.

Does it handle flat-rate techs?

Yes. Clock time feeds the payroll export for hourly and flat-rate techs: hourly techs on clock hours, flat-rate techs on flagged hours. See Reports, payroll & QuickBooks.
