Texting, calls & email

Every text, call and email on the customer’s record.

One inbox per customer for texts, emails and report replies. Connect Shop Cloud and your own Twilio number for business texting, calls that ring every phone, voicemail, missed-call texts and an optional AI receptionist.

Where calls and texts fall through

  • Customer texts land on an advisor’s personal phone.
  • Calls missed at lunch never get returned.
  • No record of what was promised.

Every conversation lives on the customer

Texts, calls, voicemails, emails and report replies sit in one thread on the customer’s record. With Shop Cloud and your Twilio number connected, they come from your business number, not someone’s cell.

Messages

One inbox, twelve ready templates

Each customer has one conversation. Texts, emails, calls and replies from their inspection report all land in it, so whoever is at the counter can pick it up.

Twelve editable templates

Merge fields fill in the customer’s name, the vehicle, your shop and the right link. Edit the wording so it sounds like your shop.

  • Checked in
  • Status update
  • Live status link
  • Vehicle ready
  • Payment request
  • Payment receipt
  • Appointment reminder
  • Service reminder
  • Declined-work follow-up
  • Review request
  • We miss you

One conversation per customer

Filter the inbox by All, Unread or Call back. Missed calls and voicemails you haven’t returned wait under Call back.

Texts from your business number

With Twilio, texts go both ways from your shop’s number. Picture messages and delivery receipts arrive live. A first-time texter becomes a contact, and STOP and START are honored.

No Twilio? Still logged.

Without it, texts and emails open in your device’s Messages or Mail app, and each one is still logged on the customer.

Suggest reply

The AI assistant drafts an answer in the composer. You edit it and decide whether it goes. It uses your own Anthropic API key.

Calls

Calls that reach someone

Incoming calls ring every listed phone at once. A screen pop shows who’s calling, their vehicle and the open repair order.

Voicemail is recorded and transcribed. When nobody picks up, a missed-call text lets the caller reply instead of calling back.

Click-to-call rings the advisor’s phone first, then connects the customer, who sees the shop’s number.

Calling runs on Shop Cloud and your Twilio number.

AI receptionist

An AI receptionist that says it’s one

Choose when it picks up: Off, After hours, When no one answers, or Answers every call. The receptionist:

  • Greets callers as the shop’s virtual assistant, by name when the caller ID matches a customer.
  • Tells a matched caller where their vehicle stands.
  • Answers from your hours and the notes you give it.
  • Takes a message for your team.
  • Files appointment requests into Calendar → Requests.
  • Texts the booking link or directions when the caller asks.
  • Rings your phones when a caller asks for a person during business hours.

It’s instructed never to make up prices, part availability or diagnoses, and to offer an advisor callback instead. Every call lands in Messages with a summary and its transcript.

It needs Shop Cloud, your Twilio number and your own Anthropic API key. Its model has its own setting and defaults to Claude Haiku 4.5 for quick replies.

Email

Email from your own address

Estimates, invoices, receipts, updates and campaigns go out from your domain with the PDF attached, in a branded layout with your text as written.

Domain setup lists the DNS records to add, then Check DNS records confirms them.

Add the delivery webhook in Resend, and each email shows Delivered, Opened (when open tracking is on in Resend), Delayed, Bounced or Marked as spam. A bounce or spam complaint flags the address, and bulk sends skip it.

Until the domain is verified, emails open in your Mail app. Sending from your address uses Shop Cloud and Resend.

Automatic reminders

Reminders that respect the clock

Turn on automatic appointment confirmations and day-before reminders. They’re off until you do.

A signed-in device queues them, and the server sends them from your Twilio number even when no device is open.

Nothing goes out before 8 AM or after 9 PM shop time. A reminder that comes due overnight waits until 8 AM, and a text more than 6 hours late is dropped.

Server sending uses Shop Cloud and your Twilio number. Other follow-ups, like review requests, are lined up for staff to send in one pass.

Every message has a home. It lands on the customer, you answer from the same screen, and what you send stays with the job.

Good to know

  • Needs: business texting, calls and the receptionist use Shop Cloud and your own Twilio number. U.S. carriers block business texts until A2P 10DLC or toll-free verification is done, and Twilio bills per text and minute.
  • The AI receptionist also needs your Anthropic API key. Usage is billed to your Anthropic account.
  • Email from your address uses Shop Cloud, Resend (free tier and paid plans) and DNS records on your domain. Delivery and open tracking need the Resend webhook.
  • Without these, messages go out from your device’s own Messages and Mail apps and are still logged on the customer.
  • Automatic confirmations and day-before reminders are off until you turn them on.

Questions

Messaging questions

Do texts come from my number?

From the Twilio number you connect. Without one, texts open in your device’s Messages app and come from that phone.

Will reminders go out at 2 AM?

No. Nothing is sent before 8 AM or after 9 PM shop time. A reminder that comes due overnight waits until 8 AM.

Will the receptionist quote prices?

It’s set up not to. It offers to have an advisor call back with exact pricing and takes a message for your team.

Can I see if an email was opened?

Yes, once the Resend delivery webhook and open tracking are set up. Each email then shows Delivered, Opened, Delayed, Bounced or Marked as spam.
