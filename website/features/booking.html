Booking, check-in & front desk

Bookings that fit your day. Check-ins that open the RO.

A booking page that only offers times you actually have open, a key-drop QR code for after hours, and a lobby screen that answers “is it ready yet?” Each self check-in opens a repair order on your signed-in shop devices.

The front desk work is already started

Customers request a real open time for you to accept, or check themselves in so the repair order is waiting on your signed-in devices.

Online booking

A booking page that knows your schedule

Customers pick the work and a time from your own booking page. It opens in any phone’s web browser, with no app or login.

  • Real open times

    Customers choose services and see real open times: business hours minus booked appointments, up to your bookings-at-once capacity.

  • Your rules

    Slots of 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, the notice you need, up to 90 days ahead, and a message on the page.

  • You accept each one

    Requests land in Calendar → Requests on your signed-in devices. Accept one and the customer and vehicle are added or matched, the appointment is booked, and a confirmation text is ready to send.

  • Returning customers

    On the same phone, their details are filled in for them.

  • Calendar views

    Switch between day and week on the Calendar, and download appointments from Integrations as an .ics file for Google, Apple or Outlook.

  • Share it anywhere

    Link it from your website, your Google profile or a text.

Self check-in

Self check-in and key drop

Print the key-drop sign or set a tablet in the lobby. Customers check themselves in, day or night, and a repair order opens on your signed-in shop devices.

  1. Scan or tap

    Scan the QR code on the printable key-drop sign, or use the lobby tablet in kiosk mode.

    QR scanned

  2. Fill it in

    Enter their details, the vehicle (the VIN barcode can be scanned), the concern, the key tag and how they’re getting around.

    VIN scanned

  3. Sign

    Sign a diagnosis authorization with your dollar limit.

    Signed

  4. The RO opens

    A repair order opens on your signed-in shop devices, matched to the customer (by phone, then email) and vehicle (by plate or VIN), attached to today’s appointment, with the signature saved.

    Repair order opened

Lobby screen

A waiting-room screen

Run the WPI Driveline Shop Management System lobby screen on a TV from a shop device. It lists every vehicle’s status by first name and last initial, ready cars first, with rotating messages, your Wi-Fi details and the check-in QR code, and pages through long lists on its own.

  • First name and last initial only. No prices, no phone numbers.
  • Ready cars first, then the cars of customers who are waiting.
  • Open it from Front Desk → Lobby screen on a shop device.

Good to know

  • Needs: Shop Cloud for real open times, the booking inbox and self check-in, on the lobby tablet or a customer’s own phone.
  • Requests and check-ins land on your shop devices once one is signed in and open. A device checks about every minute while it’s open.
  • Open times on the booking page refresh while a signed-in shop device has the app open, and whenever you publish from Settings → Online booking.
  • Without Shop Cloud, the booking page shows your business hours and sends the request by text or email instead.
  • Confirmation and day-before reminder texts are sent by Shop Cloud from your Twilio number. They’re off until you switch them on.
  • The lobby screen runs on a shop device, not a public link. It shows the check-in QR code when self check-in is on.

Questions

Booking questions

Will it double-book us?

With Shop Cloud, the booking page offers only open times, up to your bookings-at-once capacity. You accept each request, and one that overlaps another appointment is flagged before you book it.

Can customers check in after hours?

Yes, at the key drop from their own phone, with Shop Cloud. The repair order is there the next time a shop device is signed in and open.

Can I put a Book online button on my own website?

Yes. Copy your booking link, or the ready-to-paste Book online button, from Settings → Website. You can also share the link from your Google profile or in a text.

Do appointments go to Google Calendar?

Not automatically. From Integrations, download your appointments as an .ics file and open it in Google, Apple or Outlook calendars. Download again to pick up changes.
