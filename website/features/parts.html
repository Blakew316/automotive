Parts, inventory & tires

Parts on the shelf, on the job and on order.

See what’s in stock, what’s spoken for and what’s coming. Purchase orders are tracked from order to receipt, cores until they’re credited, and a scanner in the browser finds, pulls and counts parts.

Where parts slip

  • Ordering the same part twice.
  • Cores never credited.
  • Counting inventory with a clipboard.
What changesOn the Parts page

  • An Ordered column

    Every part shows what’s already on an open purchase order, so you see it before you order it again.

  • Cores with a status

    Each core charge is tracked from owed to returned to credited, on its own Cores tab.

  • Counts by scan

    Scan a part on the shelf and type what you counted. The phone or tablet you already have is the scanner.

Inventory

Inventory that tells you what to do

Every part shows what’s on the shelf, what’s already pulled onto open jobs and what’s on order. Set a min and a max, and the status column says when to reorder.

  • Shelf, jobs and orders

    The shelf count, the parts already pulled onto open repair orders and the parts on open POs, side by side with the bin and the last time it was used.

  • Min and max, worked out

    Reorder, Low · on order, Above max or OK, from your min, your max and what’s already ordered. One tap filters to low stock.

  • Values and CSV

    Value tiles at cost for parts, tires, batteries, and fluids & supplies. Export the whole list to CSV.

Parts can be transferred between locations when you run more than one. The Maintenance specs tab has sourced specs for a short list of common models (Silverado 1500, F-150, Accord, Civic, CR-V, Camry): each value was checked against an owner’s manual, an OEM parts catalog or a retailer’s fitment page, and the source is linked.

Barcode scanning

Scan instead of type

Scan UPC/EAN, Code 128 and QR barcodes to find parts, pull them onto an RO, receive POs box by box and count inventory.

VIN barcodes (Code 39, Code 128, Data Matrix, QR) scan in the VIN decoder, the vehicle form and self check-in (self check-in needs Shop Cloud).

  • Find a part
  • Pull it onto an RO
  • Receive a PO
  • Count the shelf

It uses the browser’s built-in detector, or a decoder bundled for iPhone/Safari and Firefox. A photo of the barcode or typing the code also works.

Supplier lookup

Supplier lookup, already filled in

Opens RockAuto, NAPA, O’Reilly, AutoZone and Advance with the vehicle and part search filled in, plus OEM parts stores by make.

PartsTech, Nexpart and WORLDPAC speedDIAL are links.

Pricing, stock and fitment come from the supplier’s own site, and you order there on your own account. RockAuto opens its catalog for the vehicle, since its search takes part numbers only.

Good to know

  • Ordering isn’t built into WPI Driveline Shop Management System. Supplier sites open with your search filled in, and you order on your own accounts there.
  • No live supplier data: there’s no live pricing, stock check or electronic ordering inside the app. POs are tracked in the app.
  • Scanning uses the device’s camera through the browser. If the camera isn’t available, use a photo of the barcode or type the code.
  • Transfers between locations need more than one location set up.
  • Report-link picks (tire choices and approvals) need Shop Cloud and land on the shop’s signed-in devices, which check about every minute while open. Picks on the counter tablet work without it.
  • Needs: nothing extra on one device. To keep stock, POs and cores the same on every device, use Shop Cloud.

Questions

Parts questions

Can I order from my suppliers inside the app?

No. Lookup opens their sites pre-filled, and you order there on your own account. The purchase order is tracked in the app.

Does scanning need special hardware?

No. It uses the device camera, a photo of the barcode or typing the code.

Can I see parts tied up on jobs?

Yes, in the On jobs column of the inventory table on a computer or tablet. It counts parts already pulled onto open repair orders.
