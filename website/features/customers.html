Bring them back for the work they put off.

Follow-up lists built from the work you’ve already done: oil service due, declined work, customers you haven’t seen and review requests. Your team sends them in one pass.

Where repeat visits slip away

  • Declined work forgotten.
  • Reminder postcards nobody reads.
  • No idea who’s overdue.

Your repair orders already know whose oil is due, who said “not now” to brakes and who hasn’t been back. WPI Driveline Shop Management System reads them and lines up the follow-ups, so nobody has to keep a list in their head.

Follow-up lists

Six follow-up lists

Marketing builds each list from your own appointments, repair orders and messages. Every card shows who’s ready now and what went out in the last 30 days, and you switch each one on or off.

Appointment confirmation

Confirms new bookings so the customer knows they’re on the schedule. Switched on, the server texts bookings made from then on, from your business number.

The day before each appointment. Switched on, the server texts it from your business number at the hour you pick (8 AM to 5 PM); otherwise it waits in the list for your team.

What the server sends, and what waits for your team

Confirmations and day-before reminders, once you switch them on, are sent by the server with Twilio (Shop Cloud). Until then they wait in the list with the others, for your team to send in one pass.

Campaigns

Campaigns, written once

Build a custom campaign by customer tag, the make they drive or when they last visited. Write the message once with merge fields like {first} and {vehicle}, and each customer gets their own name and their car on file.

Send everything at once from your business number (Twilio) or your email (Resend), with Shop Cloud. Otherwise send one tap at a time from the shop phone, as one BCC email, or export the list for a bulk tool.

Every send is logged on the customer’s thread. With Resend, addresses that bounced or marked you as spam are skipped.

One-pass sending uses Shop Cloud with your Twilio number or Resend. See Texting, calls & email.

History

The whole story on every customer and vehicle

Every repair order and message stays on the customer and the vehicle, and declined work stays on the customer’s page. Whoever picks up the phone sees what was done, at what mileage, and what’s still waiting.

Auto Diagnosis & vehicle data

Customers

Lifetime spend, visit history, recent messages, declined work to follow up and tags, on one page. Summarize with AI (Shop Cloud with your Anthropic key) gives the advisor a short summary.

Vehicles

A service timeline with mileage and lifetime spend, the vehicle’s configuration (system diagrams, parts list and repair guides) and a Diagnose button.

Recalls and record history

NHTSA recalls, complaints and crash ratings on demand: tap Check now, or turn on Always check. With Shop Cloud, every record keeps a change history with one-click restore.

Connected cars

Connected cars, with the owner’s OK

Send connect link texts the owner a link, good for 7 days, that opens Smartcar Connect. They sign in to their carmaker account and approve access.

Access is read-only: vehicle info, VIN, odometer, oil life, tire pressure, fuel and battery. There is no unlocking, starting or location.

When someone opens the vehicle and the last reading is more than 12 hours old, or taps Read now, the app reads the car: the mileage updates, and oil life of 15% or less and tires under 28 psi are flagged. A low oil-life reading puts the car on your oil-service list for your team to send, once it has had an oil service with you.

Tokens stay on your Shop Cloud server and never reach a device. The owner can disconnect any time.

Vehicle history reports

Service records for CARFAX

Get the work you’ve finished onto vehicle history reports, tied to each VIN.

Guidance and a CSV, not a direct connection

The app explains how to enroll in the free CARFAX Car Care service-shop program and exports a service-history CSV for any date range. ROs without a 17-character VIN are skipped and counted so you can fix them. Records reach CARFAX only after your shop enrolls.

Each row carries the VIN, year, make and model, service date, odometer, RO number, the service performed and your shop’s name, address and phone.

Good to know

  • Needs: one-pass texting uses Shop Cloud with Twilio, and one-pass email uses Shop Cloud with Resend. Without them, your team sends one tap at a time from the shop phone or mail app.
  • U.S. carriers block business texts until A2P 10DLC or toll-free verification is done, and Twilio bills per text. See Texting, calls & email.
  • Automatic appointment confirmations and day-before reminders are off until you turn them on.
  • Connected cars need Shop Cloud, Smartcar (free developer tier, paid beyond) and the owner’s OK. Which readings are available depends on the car’s make, model and year.
  • Smartcar’s Simulated mode is for trials with test vehicles only.
  • Summarize with AI uses Shop Cloud and your own Anthropic API key, billed to your shop.

Questions

Follow-up questions

Do follow-ups send themselves?

Only appointment confirmations and day-before reminders, once you switch them on. The server sends those from your Twilio number through Shop Cloud. Your team sends the rest in one pass from the Marketing page.

Can customers opt out?

Yes. STOP is honored: when a customer replies STOP to your business number, texts to them are blocked until they reply START.

Does connected-car access track location?

No. Access is read-only and covers vehicle info, VIN, odometer, oil life, tire pressure, fuel and battery. Location, unlocking and starting aren’t part of it.

Does it report to CARFAX automatically?

No. Once your shop enrolls in the CARFAX Car Care program, you export the service-history CSV and send it the way CARFAX asks.
