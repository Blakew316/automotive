Where the numbers hide

The month is all there, on your repair orders and time cards. Getting it out usually takes a spreadsheet, a calculator and a Sunday.

  • Retyping the month into QuickBooks.
  • Flat-rate math in a spreadsheet.
  • Not knowing your effective labor rate.
  • Comebacks nobody counts.

Counted from the work you already write up

Reports, payroll and the books all read the same repair orders, payments and clock time.

  • QuickBooks Online gets each day's sales as one balanced journal entry, through Shop Cloud and your own Intuit app.
  • Flat-rate pay is worked out from flagged hours, with labor and parts commission.
  • Effective labor rate is labor dollars over hours sold, for the period you pick.
  • Comebacks are counted by technician, with their rate and cost.

Accounting

Bookkeeping views from your own ROs

The Accounting page in WPI Driveline Shop Management System builds its numbers from the repair orders, payments and expenses already there, split into Profit & loss, Expenses, Deposits, Sales tax, and QuickBooks & exports.

Profit & loss

Income by type, parts and sublet cost, tech pay from the time clock, operating expenses by category, and net income.

Expenses, deposits & sales tax

An expense ledger. Deposits by day and payment method, with tips and surcharges separated. Sales tax by month: taxable, exempt and collected.

CSV exports

In QuickBooks Online's import layouts: invoices, payments, expenses, a balanced daily sales journal and customers. They also open in Xero, Wave or a spreadsheet.

QuickBooks Online

QuickBooks Online, posted daily

Each day's sales, payments, sales tax, tips, surcharges and Stripe fees post as one balanced journal entry.

  • Accounts are suggested from your chart of accounts.
  • Post any period by hand, or turn on daily posting of the last week through yesterday.
  • Re-posting a day updates its entry instead of doubling it (a day that hasn't changed is left as it is), and unbalanced or unmapped entries are refused.
  • Stripe fees are booked to card processing fees, so deposits match the bank.

Needs Shop Cloud and your own Intuit developer app. Daily posting runs when an owner or manager device is opened.

How payments reach the books

Daily summary

A daily summary in your inbox

The owner gets a summary email at the hour they pick, in the shop's time zone: sales, money collected, car count and ARO, what's in the shop, open estimates, receivables, tomorrow's appointments and items needing attention.

It needs Shop Cloud and Resend. The numbers come from an owner or manager device opened that day; if none was, the email says so.

Multiple locations

More than one location?

Run several addresses as one shop.

  • Each location keeps its own address, phone, sales tax and labor rate, and all locations share one customer list.
  • Repair orders, appointments, time, POs and inventory belong to a location.
  • Documents and texts use that location's details.
  • Reports compare locations side by side, and parts transfer between locations.

Each device picks the location it's working at, or all of them.

Good to know

  • QuickBooks sync needs Shop Cloud and your own Intuit developer app. Production keys need Intuit's review, so it runs on Intuit's sandbox until then.
  • Daily auto-posting and the daily summary's numbers depend on an owner or manager device being opened that day.
  • The daily summary email needs Shop Cloud and Resend.
  • Owner-only: Accounting and the Pay & commissions tab open only for the owner role. Managers can still review timesheets.
  • Payroll is calculated and exported. Your payroll service pays people and files taxes.

FAQ

Reporting questions

All questions
Will re-posting double my entries?

No. Re-posting a day updates that day's journal entry in QuickBooks Online instead of adding a second one.

Xero or Wave?

Use the CSV exports. Direct sync is QuickBooks Online only.

Does it run payroll?

No. It works out hours, overtime, flat-rate pay and commission, and exports them to your payroll service, which pays people and files taxes.

Can I see each tech's comebacks?

Yes. The Technicians report shows comeback rate and cost for each tech.
