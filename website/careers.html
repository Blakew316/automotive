Roles we hire for

Where you might fit in.

These are the kinds of positions a shop like ours needs to run well. Openings vary through the year, so not every role is open at any given time.

No opening listed for your role? Apply anyway. We keep applications on file and reach out when a spot opens up that fits.

Automotive Technician

Cars, light trucks, SUVs & vans

Track down problems and repair the vehicles our customers count on every day.

What you’d do

  • Diagnose warning lights, drivability and electrical problems
  • Repair brakes, suspension, steering, engines and cooling systems
  • Handle scheduled maintenance, fluids, filters and inspections
  • Write clear notes so findings are easy to explain to customers
Apply for this role: Automotive Technician

Small Engine Technician

Mowers, generators & power equipment

Keep the equipment that works hard at home and on the job running smoothly.

What you’d do

  • Service and repair push mowers, riding mowers and tractors
  • Diagnose fuel, carburetor, ignition and hard-starting problems
  • Tune up generators, pressure washers, trimmers, saws and blowers
  • Sharpen blades, replace belts and get equipment ready for the season
Apply for this role: Small Engine Technician

Service Advisor

The front counter & the phone

Be the friendly, clear voice customers talk to from drop-off to pickup.

What you’d do

  • Greet customers and listen carefully to what they’re noticing
  • Write up repair orders and schedule appointments
  • Explain findings and estimates in plain language
  • Call customers for approval before any additional work
Apply for this role: Service Advisor

Shop Assistant / Apprentice

A great way to learn the trade

Keep the shop running smoothly while you build real, hands-on skills on the job.

What you’d do

  • Keep bays, tools and equipment clean and organized
  • Help with oil changes, tire service and basic maintenance
  • Move vehicles and equipment in and out of the shop
  • Learn diagnostics and repair by working with the team
Apply for this role: Shop Assistant / Apprentice

What we value

What we look for in a teammate.

Experience matters, and so does attitude. Whether you’ve been wrenching for years or you’re just starting out, these are the things we care about most.

Pride in the work

You’d rather do it right than do it twice, and you check your work before it leaves the bay.

Honesty

You recommend what’s needed, say so when something can wait and own it when you make a mistake.

Clear communication

You can explain what you found in plain language, to teammates and to customers.

Always learning

Vehicles and equipment keep changing. You’re curious, ask good questions and like figuring things out.

Teamwork

You pitch in when the shop gets busy and share what you know with the people around you.

Safe habits

Lifts, jacks, tools and spinning blades get respect every time, for your sake and everyone else’s.
