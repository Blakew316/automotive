Who it’s for

For businesses that run on wheels and engines.

If your work depends on vehicles or power equipment, downtime costs you. Here’s who we help keep moving.

Contractors & trades

Pickups and vans loaded with tools and materials, kept ready for the next job site.

Landscaping crews

The trucks that haul the crew, plus the mowers, trimmers and blowers they unload. We service both.

Delivery & service vans

High-mileage vans that put in long days and can’t afford a surprise breakdown on the route.

Property management

Maintenance vehicles, plus the mowers, blowers and generators that keep properties looking their best.

Nonprofits & community groups

Vans and vehicles that carry volunteers, meals and supplies, kept safe and dependable.

Tell us about it

What we offer

Fleet care that works around your work.

Planned maintenance catches small problems before they park a truck. When something does break, we coordinate the fix with you.

Honest timing. Some repairs depend on parts and on what we find, so we won’t promise a turnaround we can’t keep. We will tell you what to expect and keep you posted.

Preventive maintenance schedules

A service plan for each vehicle and machine, based on manufacturer recommendations and how hard you actually work it.

Repairs coordinated around your workday

We plan drop-offs and repair timing with you, so a vehicle is out of service when it hurts least.

One point of contact

One number to call, and a contact who knows your vehicles, your preferences and how you like approvals handled.

Clear records for every unit

Service history for every vehicle and piece of equipment: what was done, when, and what’s coming due.

And small engine repair

Vehicles and equipment, under one roof.

We don’t stop at the truck. The zero-turn on the trailer and the trimmers in the bed can come in on the same trip, with the same contact and the same records.

  • One drop-off for the truck and the equipment it hauls
  • One set of records across vehicles and machines
  • Equipment tune-ups planned before your busy season
  • One call when something breaks, whatever it is

How it works

Four steps to fewer surprises.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your fleet

    Send the form or call. Tell us what you run, how many and how hard they work.

  2. 2

    Build a maintenance plan

    Together we map out service for each vehicle and machine, based on manufacturer schedules and your usage.

  3. 3

    Schedule around your day

    Service is planned for times that keep your crews moving, and we call before any added work.

  4. 4

    Records you can use

    Every visit is documented by unit, so you always know what was done and what’s next.
