Switching

Switching? Bring your data.

Import customers and vehicles, or parts inventory, from any CSV export or by pasting from a spreadsheet.

Columns are matched by common header names such as Customer, Cell, Phone, VIN, Part number and Qty on hand. Export a CSV from your current system (Shopmonkey, Tekmetric, Mitchell 1, ALLDATA Manage, Shop-Ware, NAPA TRACS, RO Writer, QuickBooks) or a spreadsheet, then check or change any match and see a preview before you import.

Customers already on file (same phone or email) aren’t added twice, and neither are vehicles whose VIN is already on file. A part number you already stock updates that part’s quantity and cost. A sample file shows the layout.

Past repair orders aren’t imported; keep your old system’s records for reference.

After import

  • Labor rate, tax and shop supplies

    Your default labor rate, your sales tax, and shop supplies as a percent of labor with a dollar cap.

  • Canned jobs and the parts matrix

    Your menu of canned jobs with set hours, and parts markup in tiers by cost.

  • Inspection template

    Start from the 37-point multi-point template and edit it to match how your techs work.

  • Team roles, PINs and logins

    Owner, shop manager, service advisor and technician. Each person’s own login uses Shop Cloud.

  • Locations

    Each location keeps its own address, phone, sales tax and labor rate.

  • Booking hours and capacity

    Slot length, notice needed, how far ahead people can book, and how many bookings at once. With Shop Cloud, requests land in the app and booked times are left out; without it, the booking page shows your hours and sends requests by text or email.

  • Connect what you want

    Stripe, Twilio, Resend and QuickBooks, on your own accounts, through Shop Cloud.

  • Install on every device

    Add it from the browser to the counter PC, the bay iPad and your phone.

None of it has to happen in one sitting, and each setting can be changed later.

What about pricing?

We don’t publish prices. Tell us about your shop in a demo request, and ask how Shop Cloud is set up and billed for you.

Services you connect (Stripe, Twilio, Resend, Anthropic, Smartcar, Intuit) bill your shop directly at their own rates.

Good to know

  • Sample shop: it runs in your browser and keeps its data on this device. If the device already has a shop set up, the app opens that shop instead.
  • Import: customers and vehicles, or parts inventory, from a CSV file or a spreadsheet paste. Past repair orders aren’t imported.
  • Needs: Shop Cloud to share records between devices and for each person’s own login. Texting, card payments, email, QuickBooks, connected cars and AI also run on your shop’s own Twilio, Stripe, Resend, Intuit, Smartcar and Anthropic accounts.
  • Prices: we don’t publish prices. Ask in your request.
  • The form: we need your name and email, plus your shop name for a demo request. Everything else is optional.

Questions

Demo questions

All questions
Do I need to install anything to try it?

No. The sample shop runs in your browser, with no sign-in. It keeps its data on that device.

Can I run my old system alongside?

Yes. Import is a one-way CSV, so your old system keeps all of its records and nothing is sent back to it.

What happens to what I send?

We use it only to reply to you. See the privacy page for the details.
