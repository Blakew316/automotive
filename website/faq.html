Getting started

4 questions
What does it cost?

We don’t publish prices. Tell us about your shop in a demo request, and ask how Shop Cloud is set up and billed for you. Services you connect (Stripe, Twilio, Resend, Anthropic, Smartcar, Intuit) bill your shop directly at their own rates.

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. Open the sample shop: Main Street Auto Service, a made-up shop with customers, cars, repair orders and a full workflow board. It opens in your browser with no sign-up, so click anything. On a device that already has your shop, the link opens your shop instead. Changes you make in the sample stay in that browser.

When you want to see it with your own questions, request a demo.

What do I need to run it?

A current web browser on the devices you already have: the counter PC, a tablet in the bay, your phone.

Shop Cloud is optional. It links your devices and runs the server side; without it, each device keeps its own records. Outside accounts such as Stripe or Twilio are optional too, and only needed for the features that use them.

Can I import my repair-order history?

Not today. You can import customers and vehicles, and your parts inventory, from a CSV export or a spreadsheet. Past repair orders don’t come across, so keep your old system’s records for lookups.

The importer is built for exports from Shopmonkey, Tekmetric, Mitchell 1, ALLDATA Manage, Shop-Ware, NAPA TRACS, RO Writer, QuickBooks and Excel or Google Sheets. Columns are matched for you and previewed before import, and duplicates are caught by phone, email, VIN and part number.

Devices & offline

5 questions
Is it in the App Store or Google Play?

No. It isn’t a native store app. It installs from the browser: on iPhone or iPad, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen with its own icon and works offline.

On Android, Mac and PC, use the browser’s install option.

How to install it

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, Macs and Windows PCs, in a current browser. A typical setup is the counter PC, a tablet in each bay, your phone, and a TV in the waiting room for the lobby screen.

On iPhone it has a bottom tab bar and sheets you swipe down. On a keyboard, ⌘K or Ctrl+K opens the command palette.

Phones, tablets and PCs

Does it work when the internet drops?

Yes. Each device keeps the shop’s records, photos and video in its own storage, so it opens and keeps working with no connection. With Shop Cloud, edits made offline stay on the device and sync when it reconnects.

A few things wait for the internet. Photos taken on another device download the first time you open them, vehicle data for each make downloads the first time it’s used and then stays on the device, and texts, card payments and NHTSA lookups need a connection.

How offline works

Can several devices share one shop?

Yes, with Shop Cloud and shared shop data turned on. Every device works on the same repair orders, customers and schedule, and changes show up on the others live.

If two people edit the same record, the edits merge field by field. Lines added to the same RO on two devices are both kept. Without Shop Cloud, each device keeps its own separate records.

Can I put a status screen in the waiting room?

Yes. The lobby screen runs from a shop device: open it full screen in the browser of a shop computer or tablet connected to the TV. It lists every car’s status by first name and last initial, ready cars first, with your messages, Wi-Fi details and, with Shop Cloud, the self check-in QR code. Long lists page through on their own.

The lobby screen

Shop Cloud

3 questions
What is Shop Cloud?

Shop Cloud is the cloud side of WPI Driveline Shop Management System: a private Supabase project that holds one shop’s shared records, logins and files and runs its server functions.

It adds shared data across devices, staff logins and two-step sign-in, share links and online approvals, the live status page, the booking inbox and remote check-in, the fleet portal, change history and nightly backups, and encrypted storage for your service keys.

How Shop Cloud works

Do outside services need Shop Cloud?

Yes, all except the built-in NHTSA vehicle data, which needs only an internet connection. Texting and calls (Twilio), card payments (Stripe), email from your own address (Resend), QuickBooks sync (Intuit), connected cars (Smartcar) and the AI features (Anthropic) all run through Shop Cloud. Their keys are stored encrypted on your Shop Cloud server, and no device can read them back.

What comes back, like a payment, an approval or a check-in, lands on your signed-in shop devices. A device checks about every minute while it’s open.

Services it works with

How is Shop Cloud set up and billed?

Ask about it in your demo request: tell us about your shop, and ask how Shop Cloud would be set up and billed for you. Outside services you connect bill your shop directly at their own rates.

Customers & texting

4 questions
Do my customers need an app?

No. Customer pages open in any phone’s browser with no login or app: the inspection report with approvals, live status, booking, self check-in, pay and the fleet portal.

All of them except booking need Shop Cloud, and online pay also needs your own Stripe account. Without Shop Cloud, the booking page sends the customer’s request to you by text or email, and for approvals you hand the customer the counter tablet or send the report as one .html file.

Reports and approvals

Do I need Twilio to text customers?

No. Without Twilio, texts and emails open in the device’s own Messages or Mail app, and they’re still logged on the customer.

With your shop’s own Twilio number, connected through Shop Cloud, you get two-way business texting, calls that ring up to five of your phones, voicemail, missed-call texts and, with your Anthropic API key, the AI receptionist. U.S. carriers block business texts until A2P 10DLC or toll-free verification is done, and Twilio bills per text and per minute.

Texting, calls & email

When do automatic texts go out?

Two kinds of scheduled text go out on their own, sent by your Shop Cloud server with Twilio: appointment confirmations and day-before reminders. Both stay off until you switch them on. Scheduled texts never go out before 8 AM or after 9 PM shop time; anything due then waits until 8 AM, and a text more than 6 hours late is dropped.

Replies to a customer are different: the missed-call text (on once your Twilio number is connected) and the optional after-hours auto-reply go out right away, whatever the hour. Review requests, service-due reminders, declined-work follow-ups and “we miss you” texts are lined up for your staff to send in one pass.

Quiet hours

Does the AI receptionist quote prices?

Only prices you write into its notes, such as a diagnostic fee. Its instructions say never to make up prices, diagnoses, repair times or policies, so for any other price it says a service advisor will follow up and offers to take a message.

With the default greeting it says it’s the shop’s virtual assistant (if you write your own greeting, keep that line). It answers from your shop’s hours and notes, takes appointment requests (they land in Calendar → Requests on your signed-in shop devices), can text the caller the booking link or directions, and during open hours rings your staff when a caller asks for a person. It needs Shop Cloud, your Twilio number and your shop’s Anthropic API key, and its replies on calls count toward the same monthly AI request limit as the assistant. Staff review the AI assistant’s drafts before they reach a customer; the receptionist is the one exception, because it talks to callers directly.

The AI receptionist

Payments & books

5 questions
Which card readers work with it?

Stripe’s internet-connected smart readers: the Stripe Reader S700 and the BBPOS WisePOS E. “Take payment” sends the amount, tip and surcharge to the reader. The customer taps, inserts or swipes, and the payment is recorded on the RO.

Bluetooth readers and Tap to Pay on a phone aren’t supported; payments taken that way are recorded by hand. Readers need your own Stripe account and Shop Cloud.

Ways to get paid

Where does the money go?

Payments go straight to your own Stripe account at Stripe’s standard rates. Online payments (which need Shop Cloud) land on the RO with the card brand, last four digits and Stripe’s fee, on your signed-in shop devices. A device checks about every minute while it’s open.

Cash, checks, ACH, financing, warranty and fleet-account payments are recorded on the RO too.

Payments & card readers

Can customers finance a repair?

The app can show an “as low as” monthly figure on bigger estimates and on the customer report, with a link to apply through your own financing partner. You set the partner, the link, an example APR and the minimum amount.

There’s no lender integration. Applications, approvals and final terms happen with your lender.

Customer financing

Does it run payroll?

No. It calculates hours, overtime, flat-rate hours and commission, and the owner can mark each pay period approved. It exports an hours-import CSV for Gusto, ADP, Paychex or QuickBooks Payroll, a detailed payroll CSV and printable timecards. Your payroll service pays people and files taxes.

Payroll export

Does it work with QuickBooks?

Yes, with QuickBooks Online. Each day’s sales, payments, sales tax, tips, surcharges and Stripe fees post as one balanced journal entry, and re-posting a day updates it instead of doubling it.

Direct sync needs Shop Cloud and your own Intuit developer app. Production keys need Intuit’s review, so it runs against a sandbox company until then, and daily posting runs when an owner or manager device is opened that day. CSV exports in QuickBooks Online’s import layouts work without any of that.

QuickBooks sync

Data & security

5 questions
Where is my shop’s data kept?

On each device, in its browser storage, which is why it works offline. With Shop Cloud and shared shop data on, it also lives in a private Supabase project for your one shop, and every device syncs through it.

Without Shop Cloud, the records live only in that one browser. Clearing the browser’s data erases them, so export a backup file now and then.

Where your data lives

Is my shop backed up?

With Shop Cloud, the whole shop is backed up every night, and the last 14 backups are kept. You can download any of them, and the owner can restore the shop to one; the current state is backed up first.

Every RO, customer and vehicle also keeps six months of change history: who changed what, when and from which device, with one-click restore. Without Shop Cloud, export a backup file by hand.

History and backups

Can staff use two-step sign-in?

Yes. It works with authenticator apps such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Authy or the phone’s built-in password app. It’s enforced on the server, not just on screen: with it on, a stolen password alone can’t reach data, files or server functions.

The owner can require two-step for chosen roles and reset it for someone who lost their phone. Logins and two-step need Shop Cloud.

Sign-in and two-step

What can each person on my staff see?

Roles decide which pages each person sees on a device. The Owner sees everything. A Shop manager runs the shop without accounting or payroll. A Service advisor works the counter: ROs, customers, messages, scheduling and parts. A Technician gets the tech clock, workflow, ROs and technical info. On a shared tablet, people switch with an optional 4-digit PIN.

Roles shape the screens; they aren’t a lock on the data. Any staff login can read the shop’s records. The server guards specific actions: making and restoring backups, changing keys and connecting Stripe are owner-only, and refunds and QuickBooks are for the owner or a manager (connecting QuickBooks is owner-only).

Roles in detail

Does it have security certifications?

No. We don’t claim SOC 2, PCI DSS or HIPAA certification. Card numbers are handled by Stripe Checkout and Stripe’s card readers and never pass through the app.

How payments stay with Stripe

In the shop

6 questions
Can I order parts inside the app?

No. Supplier lookup opens RockAuto, NAPA, O’Reilly, AutoZone, Advance and OEM parts stores with the vehicle and part search filled in, and PartsTech, Nexpart and WORLDPAC are links to your accounts there. There’s no live pricing, stock check or electronic ordering inside the app.

Purchase orders are tracked in the app from order to receipt. Receiving one adds stocked parts to inventory and marks parts ordered for a repair order as received on that RO.

Supplier lookup

Does it report service records to CARFAX?

Not directly. Your shop enrolls in the free CARFAX Car Care service-shop program; the app explains the steps and exports a service-history CSV for any date range. Repair orders without a 17-character VIN are skipped and counted so you can fix them.

CARFAX export

Is Auto Diagnosis a diagnosis?

No. It gives leads to verify: recalls that may cover the concern, what other owners of the same model report to NHTSA, what fixed it at your shop before, and what the codes mean.

Symptom checks are general, not make-specific. Recalls match by year, make and model, not an open-recall status for one VIN. NHTSA lookups need internet, and the optional AI test plan needs Shop Cloud and your Anthropic API key. A tech still confirms every lead.

Auto Diagnosis

Does it replace OEM service information?

No. The vehicle database’s diagrams, parts lists and repair guides are generated to represent each configuration. Inferred items are labelled “typical”, and torque values, capacities, pinouts and wire colors are left to factory service information.

OEM portals are links, and their subscriptions are separate. Keep ALLDATA, Mitchell or OEM information for those specs.

The vehicle database

Can I run more than one location?

Yes. Each location has its own address, phone, sales tax and labor rate, and all of them share one customer list. Repair orders, appointments, time, purchase orders and inventory belong to a location, and each device picks the location it works at, or all of them.

Estimates, invoices and texts use that location’s details. Reports compare locations side by side, and parts can be transferred between them.

Multiple locations

Can I bill fleets on account?

Yes. Business accounts get terms from due on receipt to Net 60, a credit limit, a not-to-exceed amount per visit, required PO numbers and unit numbers. Invoices print the PO, unit, terms and due date, and receivables aging and statements are built in.

With Shop Cloud, the fleet manager gets a private portal link: each unit’s maintenance status, what’s in the shop and open invoices.

Fleet & business accounts
