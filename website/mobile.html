Install

Install it in a minute

Open the app in the browser on each device and add it to the Home Screen or the dock. From then on it opens full screen, with its own icon. With Shop Cloud, sign in once on each device and the shop’s data loads.

On iPhone and iPad

01

Open the app in Safari

Use the same address on every device in the shop.

02

Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen

Share is the square with the arrow. The icon lands on the Home Screen and opens full screen, without Safari’s bars.

03

Open it from the Home Screen

With Shop Cloud, sign in once and your shop’s data loads onto that iPhone or iPad. Without it, each device keeps its own copy. To move the records, export a backup in Settings → Data and import it on the other device.

Offline-first

Works with no connection

Each device keeps the shop’s records, so it opens and works offline, and vehicle data is saved as you use it. Edits made offline survive reloads.

With Shop Cloud:

  • The counter, bay tablets and your phone update live.
  • Edits made offline are sent when the device reconnects.
  • Photos from other devices download the first time you open them.
  • Two edits to the same record merge field by field, and lines added to the same RO on two devices are both kept.
  • RO and PO numbers created offline are renumbered the same way everywhere.

Good to know

  • Not a store app: it isn’t an App Store or Google Play app. It installs from the browser.
  • Without Shop Cloud: data lives only in that browser, and clearing browser data erases it, so export backups from Settings → Data.
  • Needs: syncing between devices needs Shop Cloud.
  • Large files: with Shop Cloud, photo and video files over 50 MB aren’t uploaded, so they stay on the device that took them.
  • On iPhone and iPad: the Home Screen app keeps its own data, separate from Safari, so add it to the Home Screen before you start entering work. Without Shop Cloud, export backups from Settings → Data.

Questions

App questions

Is it in the App Store?

No. It installs from the browser with Add to Home Screen or Install app, so there’s no store listing. A notice appears in the app when a newer version is published.

Which browsers?

A current Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Dictation needs a browser with speech recognition: Chrome, Edge or Safari. If your browser has no Install option, the app still runs in a normal tab.

Does the barcode scanner work on iPhone?

Yes, with a decoder bundled for Safari. The camera reads part barcodes and VIN barcodes, and a photo of the barcode or typing the code works too.

What happens if we lose internet?

Keep working. Each device has the shop’s records, and edits made offline stay on the device. With Shop Cloud, they sync to the other devices when it reconnects.
